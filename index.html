Physics

The object's weight is mg. On the ramp, only part of gravity acts down the slope:

Fgravity = mg sin(θ)

The ramp also produces a normal force:

N = mg cos(θ)

With friction coefficient μ, friction opposing the motion is:

Ffriction = μmg cos(θ)

Therefore, pulling at constant speed requires:

Fpull = mg(sin(θ) + μcos(θ))

For comparison, lifting vertically at constant speed requires:

Flift = mg
